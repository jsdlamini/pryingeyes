\chapter{Design decisions}
\label{app:decisions}

These are the points where the specification left a choice open, or where the build departs from it. The full list, with reasons, is in \fpath{docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md}. Items marked \textbf{(review)} need the product owner's confirmation.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{4.4cm}p{10.8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\thead{Decision} & \thead{Why} \\
\midrule\endhead
Drizzle instead of Prisma \textbf{(review)} & Prisma's migration engine is a binary that couldn't be downloaded in the build environment, so it couldn't be tested. Drizzle is pure TypeScript with plain SQL migrations on the same PostgreSQL. \\
Codes shown once \textbf{(review)} & Only HMAC fingerprints are stored, as rule 4 requires. A lost link is replaced by rotating the token. The alternative is storing codes encrypted, which would let owners copy them again. \\
\code{APP_ENV} separate from \code{NODE_ENV} & \code{next start} always sets \code{NODE_ENV=production}, even on a desktop. \code{APP_ENV} decides whether development stand-ins are allowed. \\
CAPTCHA before token lookup & A CAPTCHA failure after the lookup would reveal that the code exists. \\
Per-caller in-flight cap of 2 & Without it, a burst of parallel guesses was evaluated before the lockout applied, giving 99.90\% blocked and failing the over-99.9\% bar. With it: 99.95\%. \\
Generated handles & Handles are visible to everyone a person contacts, so the default is random (\code{adjective_noun_NN}) and never derived from the email, name or phone. People can still type their own. \\
Chat by polling (3 s) & Works through any proxy with a stateless server. The message API is transport-independent, so WebSockets or server-sent events can be added later. \\
Region is country-level & Taken from the edge's country header, or the caller's phone country. No GeoIP database is shipped. \\
Panic without step-up & Revoking is always safe; re-issuing afterwards needs a fresh second factor. \\
Development outbox stores masked numbers & Even development databases pass the plaintext scan. \\
Resend for email, via its REST API & No SDK or SMTP dependency. Idempotent retries on 429 and 5xx only; text and HTML bodies; category tags; addresses never logged. The test sender is refused outside development. \\
Daily anchors for completed days only & A day's head is final only once the day ends. \\
Phase 2 seams & Tier 4 is in the schema but can't be selected; the KMS interface and the chat encryption module are isolated for later replacement. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
