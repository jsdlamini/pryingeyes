\section{HTTP API}

All endpoints are under \fpath{/api} and exchange JSON. Every \code{POST}, \code{PATCH} and \code{DELETE} must come from the same origin; cross-site requests get \code{403 bad_origin}. Errors have the shape \texttt{\{"error": code, "message": text\}}. Responses are never cached.

\section{Glossary}

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{3.6cm}p{11.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\thead{Term} & \thead{Meaning} \\
\midrule\endhead
Token / code & A random identifier an owner shares instead of a phone number: a 16-character link code or a 10-character short code. \\
Decode gate & The checks a caller must pass before any outcome is delivered. \\
Tier & The protection level of a token (1 Open, 2 Gated, 3 Consent, 4 Vault in Phase 2). \\
Outcome & What the caller gets after the gate: a chat, an in-app call, or a reveal if the code has a number. \\
Watermarked reveal & The number drawn into an image stamped with the viewer's handle and the time, shown for 60 seconds. \\
Step-up & A fresh second-factor check before a sensitive action. \\
Passkey & A WebAuthn credential unlocked with the device's screen lock. Phishing-resistant. \\
TOTP & Six-digit codes from an authenticator app (RFC 6238). \\
Envelope encryption & Data encrypted with a per-user data key, which is itself encrypted (wrapped) by a key-encryption key (KEK). \\
Blind index & A keyed hash that allows equality lookups on encrypted values. \\
Pepper & A server-side secret mixed into a hash, here the token fingerprint. \\
Crypto-shredding & Making data unreadable by deleting its key. \\
Hash chain & A log in which each entry includes the hash of the previous one, so changes are detectable. \\
Anchor & A day's final chain hash, written to write-once storage. \\
Evidence pack & A signed export of an owner's audit trail for platforms or police. \\
Break-glass & Exceptional administrator actions that need two people (dual control). \\
E.164 & The international phone-number format, e.g. \code{+26876123456}. \\
OWASP ASVS & Application Security Verification Standard; Level 2 is the go-live bar. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
