\chapter{The system's screens}
\label{ch:screens}

These are real screenshots of PryingEyes, taken by the script \fpath{tests/screens/capture.spec.ts}. It walks through the system as real people would: an owner creates codes and a group, a caller signs up from a code, they chat, a code is shared in the chat, a call rings, someone asks to join a group and an admin lets them in. Re-take them at any time with:

\begin{lstlisting}
cd mobile && npx expo export --platform web --output-dir dist-web && cd ..
npx playwright test -c playwright.screens.config.ts
\end{lstlisting}

Website screens are 1280\,$\times$\,800 (desktop). Phone app screens are 412\,$\times$\,915, a typical Android phone; they come from the app's web build, which renders the same React Native screens as the installed app. Handles such as \code{@quiet\_heron\_45} are generated, as they would be for real people.

\section{Look and feel}

Since version 0.9 the website and the phone app share one visual style, taken from the \emph{Talk Sense} social app concept on Dribbble. The same values are defined twice, in \fpath{src/app/globals.css} for the website and \fpath{mobile/src/ui/theme.ts} for the app, so the two stay in step.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.27\linewidth}p{0.68\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Element} & \textbf{Treatment} \\
\midrule
Background & A soft blush-to-lilac ``sky'' gradient (\code{\#F6E8EF} to \code{\#E8D8EC}); a deep plum version in dark mode. \\
Cards & Frosted, translucent white with a 22-pixel radius and a faint pink shadow. \\
Type & Merriweather (serif) for page titles, names, codes and the call timer; Inter (sans-serif) for everything you read and type. Both are bundled with the app and the site, so no font is fetched from another server. \\
Buttons & Ink-black pills (\code{\#1C1A1F}) with white text for the main action; white pills with a thin border for the others. \\
Accent & Magenta (\code{\#B5166A} for text, \code{\#E0218A} for badges and highlights), chosen to pass WCAG AA contrast on white. \\
Chips and choices & White pills; the selected one turns ink-black. \\
Chat & Rounded bubbles: yours ink-black, theirs white, with the time tucked inside. The composer is a round code button and a pill-shaped ``Type a message here\ldots'' field. \\
Status avatars & Rounded squares rather than circles, ringed in magenta when there is something new. \\
Calls & A plum-to-ink panel with a pale pink serif timer and round controls. \\
Icons & One set of line icons (24-pixel grid, 1.75 stroke) on both the website and the app. \emph{Send} and \emph{Post} are a see-through magenta paper plane; \emph{report} is a flag, \emph{block} a struck circle, \emph{delete} a bin, \emph{sign out} an exit arrow; calls use round green and red buttons. Icon-only buttons keep a spoken name (for example ``Send''), so screen readers and the tests still find them; actions whose meaning needs words, such as \emph{Let in} or \emph{Leave group}, keep their label beside the icon. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
